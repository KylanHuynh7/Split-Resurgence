=== FS vs CH MixedLM coefficient comparison ===

Sample sizes (pitcher-seasons with >= 100 pitches):
  FS: 239 pitcher-seasons, 111 pitchers, 26 focal-roster rows, 8 NPB-flagged rows
  CH: 1166 pitcher-seasons, 520 pitchers, 3 focal-roster rows, 0 NPB-flagged rows

Fixed-effects coefficients side-by-side:
  Intercept              FS: -5.371 (p=0.274)        CH: -3.174 (p=0.101)
  npb_years              FS: +0.101 (p=0.314)        CH: DROPPED
  mlb_seasons_in         FS: -0.053 (p=0.101)        CH: +0.002 (p=0.890)
  fastball_velo_mean     FS: +0.053 (p=0.295)        CH: +0.029 (p=0.148)
  usage_share            FS: +3.148 (p=0.002)        CH: +2.340 (p=0.000)
  is_novelty_year        FS: -0.865 (p=0.027)        CH: -0.127 (p=0.464)

--- Reading the comparison ---
  npb_years is DROPPED on CH because none of the 5 focal NPB-developed
  pitchers (Yamamoto, Ohtani, Imanaga, Sasaki, Senga) reached the
  100-changeup threshold in any season. The Roberts hypothesis is
  about splitters specifically; this data confirms NPB-developed
  pitchers do not throw changeups in volumes large enough to test
  whether NPB development would generalize to other off-speed pitches.

  For the remaining predictors, similar coefficient signs and
  magnitudes between FS and CH would mean the effect is general
  off-speed behavior. Different magnitudes mean it is specific to
  the splitter — those are the more interesting findings.
